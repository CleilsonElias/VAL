# Attribution and scope

VAL (Valued Asymmetric Lambda) is the architectural proposal of
**Cleilson Elias de Sousa**, in the manuscript dated 10 May 2026,
*VAL: Valued Asymmetric Lambda - Architecture of Artificial Cognition in the
Domain of Hyperfocus*. This reference software was prepared on 11 November
2026 with AI-assisted implementation and review.

This is a new implementation from the supplied manuscript, not a recovered or
certified copy of code used to produce that manuscript. No original training
code, raw experimental data, checkpoints, or validation logs were supplied.

The MIT license covers this repository's newly created code and documentation.
The original PDF and third-party articles are not redistributed or relicensed.
The author's revised manuscript is included with authorization to publish this
project; the software MIT license does not relicense the original manuscript.
The frozen 0.1.0 study core is preserved separately from the 0.2.0 runtime.
The paper's biological explanations, theorem, and numerical predictions are
assessed in `docs/SCIENTIFIC_AUDIT.md`; inclusion here does not certify them.

Software profiles A1/A2/A3 are parameter configurations. They are not diagnostic
categories or faithful models of autistic people, savants, creativity, or flow.
The software does not measure consciousness or create latent abilities merely
by lowering lambda. It controls access to supplied external-memory episodes.
